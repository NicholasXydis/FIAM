## 4. The cost model (settled; do not regress to a flat rate)

- Engine (`engine/nautilus_backend.Costs`): half-spread from real closing bid/ask
  (21-day median), impact `Y x sigma_daily x $ x sqrt($ / ADV)` per fill with
  trades paced over 5 days, Y = 1.0, dated IBKR/SEC/FINRA/CAT fees, borrow by
  short-interest tier. Same family as v4's `fiam/costs.py` (v4 charges the
  monthly trade at once, so slightly harsher). v4 pays 0.87% spread + 2.24% impact;
  our clean book paid 0.34% + 2.5%.
- Fast screen (`research/stage2.simulate`): per name `|trade| x (half-spread +
  sigma x sqrt(trade $ / (5 x ADV)) + 1 bp)` + 0.33%/yr borrow, half-spread =
  `bidaskhl_21d` x v4's size-group ratio (`submission/models/spreads.json`,
  calibrated 2015–2020), `size_grp` merged from `data.PANEL`.
- Y = 0.5 is the stated sensitivity (as v4 reports); headline stays Y = 1.

## 5. Next build: two-step construction (user's proposal, 2026-10-26)

**Answer to the user's question.** It can be done, and the engine is already
partly like this: E5 stage 0 picks names and signs, stage 2 solves weights on
them. But stage 2 trades return against risk in one objective with a 6% vol
cap, and lesson 96 showed it drifting to calm, large, mid-ranked names (the
score's beta loading −0.5/−0.63). The user's version differs in one way that
matters: **step 1 fixes the names by expected return, and step 2 may only
reweight inside that set, with expected return held at a floor.** The forecast
then cannot be diluted toward the middle of the ranking.

What to expect, stated honestly: step 2 lowers vol and beta for the same
expected return, so it raises Sharpe; it does not create alpha the selected
names lack. The fast screen's books were already a crude version (hard top/
bottom-n selection, equal weights, beta-split gross) and made +2%/yr over cash,
with the long leg negative. So the two-step must be paired with the fixes the
evidence points to: slower scores (smoothing) and a turnover-aware step 2. It
should be judged on 2017–2020 first (D16), then one disclosed 2021–2026 run.

**Start from the paper (HHRZ 2018, `external/fm_appendix/HHRZ_2018_wp.txt`
end of §2, lines 1078–1085).** Its portfolios sort stocks into forecast deciles with
NYSE breakpoints and hold the top and bottom deciles: value-weighted (the main
case), equal-weighted excluding microcaps (below the NYSE 20th percentile), or
equal-weighted. Its forecasts are fitted for value-weighted MSFE (WLS on `me`),
which is what our port does. So step 1 = the paper's sort; step 2 = our risk model.

Spec:
1. **Step 1, selection (return only).** Score = walk-forward E-LASSO MaInf
   (`walkforward/month` or `month_holdable`), optionally smoothed over 3–6
   months (past only). Deciles by NYSE breakpoints within the eligible pool
   (shorts need min(me, me_company) >= $500m). Long = D10, short = D1, capped to
   keep 200–500 names; held names that are still in D9/D2 stay (hysteresis
   against churn). Variants to pre-register: VW vs EW-ex-micro (the paper's two
   main cases).
2. **Step 2, weights (risk, with a return floor).** On the fixed set S with fixed
   signs, minimise `w'Σw + λ_β (β'w)² + turnover cost(w − w_held)` subject to
   `μ'w >= ρ x μ'w_ref` (w_ref = the paper's VW or EW decile weights, ρ e.g. 0.9),
   `|w_i| <= 1.5%` (v4) or 5% (rule), gross <= 1.8, |net| <= 0.5, sector bands, and
   sign(w_i) fixed. Σ, β, μ = the same enhanced risk model, beta calibration and
   calibrated E-LASSO μ the engine already uses (`risk/enhanced.py`,
   `engine/optimize.py` inputs). Then scale the whole book to the vol target
   (try 6% and v4's 12%, inside the gross cap).
3. **Prototype in the fast screen first** (`research/stage2.py` gives panel,
   per-name costs, rolling hedge and stats). Only the Σ needs adding (e.g. a
   factor model from `risk/`, or sample covariance of 252-day residuals as a
   quick stand-in). Then implement as a new `Step` in `engine/optimize.py`
   (support = step 1, QP = step 2) and run E6 on Modal on 2017–2020.
4. **Rule before running** (write it in DECISIONS as D18 first): E6 is adopted
   over the D17 winner if its 2017–2020 net is higher with |beta| <= 0.05 and both
   legs' after-beta alpha > 0. Then one disclosed 2021–2026 run.
5. Report both legs (+ = profit), costs by line, names, gross, beta, breaches.
