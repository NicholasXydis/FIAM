## 4. ADV source units — RESOLVED. Plan verdict: FIX (name the field), MEDIUM (a 21× error if misread)

Direct computation on the supplied panel (Jan-2021 projection) settles the units the readme leaves implicit:

- `dolvol` = **monthly** dollar volume in USD: `dolvol / (|prc|·tvol)` median = **1.000** exactly, with `tvol` monthly shares traded (median `dolvol` $192m/month).
- `dolvol_126d` (one of the 147) = **average daily** dollar volume over trailing 126 trading days, USD/day: median ratio to `dolvol/21` = 0.92, log-corr 0.976 (median $8.3m/day). `turnover_126d` is correspondingly a daily share-turnover rate (~1/21 of monthly `tvol/shares`).
- `dsf_mini_from_2014.csv` has exactly four columns — `permno,date,prc,daily_return` — **no volume at all**. Daily ADV therefore cannot be built from the supplied daily file; Mandate §11 (L1034–1036) routes price *levels* to Carbon Arc CA0065S / Arcana, and says nothing promising daily volume.

So the only auditable ADV in hand is monthly-cadence: `dolvol_126d` lagged one month (or `dolvol/21`), carried across the days of the following month. Plan §7 L254 ("Use lagged observed dollar volume, not monthly volume mislabeled as daily") states the right warning but never names the field — and the failure mode is severe: treating `dolvol` as daily ADV overstates capacity 21× and understates days-to-exit 21× in Plan's own constraints (L250–251: `NAV|q| ≤ p·ADV`, `NAV|w|/(p·ADV) ≤ D_max`), corrupting the §9.4 days-of-ADV exhibit (Mandate L988). **Fix:** config names `ADV ≡ lagged dolvol_126d (USD/day)`, stressed down (Plan L254), with CA0065S volume substituted only after its units/coverage pass the workbook checks. Repo evidence is consistent (`fiam/exhibits/implementation.py:37` uses `dolvol_126d` as the daily denominator) but that is corroboration, not authority. Event-day residual: only whether CA0065S offers cleaner daily volume — an upgrade question, not a blocker.

## 5. Initial-ramp count rule — UNRESOLVED (mandate silent). Plan verdict: KEEP (L256), HIGH for simulator design, with a frozen default

**Frozen default until organizers rule (either answer is presentable if disclosed identically in simulator, §9.1 min-count, and deck):** simulate an explicit funded ramp from cash over the first `D_ramp` trading days (participation-capped partial fills, formation costs charged inside OOS measurement per §9.3 with `w−=0`), report ramp days separately, and treat the 200-floor as binding on every *target* book from the first rebalance while executed holdings converge. ## 6. Numeric beta tolerance — UNRESOLVED as a compliance line; RESOLVED as statistics. Plan verdict: KEEP ±0.02 as internal ex-ante policy (L163, L168), never as the mandate line

The mandate deliberately gives no number: judges *"will estimate your realised beta ourselves, at both daily and monthly frequency, and a beta materially different from zero … will be read as a directional strategy"* (§3.3 L196–198); §9.2 requires beta ± s.e. at both frequencies (L947–951). "Materially" is judges' discretion and stays that way — no document in hand converts it to a threshold, and Plan L49 correctly classifies its own tolerance as internal policy.

What *is* computable now is what any judge can statistically see, using `SE(β̂) = σ_e/(σ_m·√T)` with realized S&P 500 vol from the repo FRED file over 2021-01–2026-08 (daily σ_ann 16.6%, T_d=1,421; monthly σ_ann 15.1%, T_m=68) and strategy residual vol scenarios (recomputed this session):

| Book resid. vol | Freq | SE(β) | 95% CI half-width | t at β=0.02 | t at β=0.10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5%/yr | daily | 0.008 | 0.016 | 2.50 | 12.5 |
| 5%/yr | monthly | 0.040 | 0.079 | 0.50 | 2.49 |
| 8%/yr | daily | 0.013 | 0.025 | 1.56 | 7.8 |
| 8%/yr | monthly | 0.064 | 0.126 | 0.31 | 1.56 |

Consequences: (i) the **daily** regression is the binding test — |β| ≳ 0.03 is detectable, so Plan's ±0.02 nominal band (L168) is sensibly inside daily detectability as an *ex-ante control* target, with the uncertainty-aware SOC `|β̂ᵀw| + z√(wᵀU_β w) ≤ ε_β` (L163) the correct robust form; (ii) the **monthly** regression cannot distinguish β=0 from β=0.08 at 5% vol — a monthly β of 0.05 with s.e. 0.04 is neither compliance nor breach, and the deck must present it as such (Plan L168: "an insignificant beta with a wide CI is weak evidence of neutrality" — keep verbatim); (iii) daily β attenuated toward zero by nonsynchronous trading in small names must not be sold as neutrality if monthly β disagrees (Dimson lead/lag sensitivity). Freeze: ex-ante target 0, internal band ±0.02, reported daily+monthly β with HAC s.e.; describe "materially ≠ 0" as the judges' daily-t test. That exhausts what is knowable pre-event.

## 7. Price vs total-return S&P 500 — RESOLVED as a choice with quantified (small) stakes. Plan verdict: FIX (name the series), LOW for beta / MEDIUM for exhibits

Mandate says only "S&P 500 excess return" (§9.2 L947), "show the S&P 500 alongside" (§3.4 L210, §9.4 L978–980) — no series, no vendor, no dividend treatment, and §11 supplies no index file. Repo practice (evidence only): FRED `SP500` price index, explicitly *"excludes dividends"* ([fiam/book.py](/home/drewclaw/Forkfiam/fiam/book.py:61), [fiam/benchmarks.py](/home/drewclaw/Forkfiam/fiam/benchmarks.py:80)).

Stakes, derived: TR − price ≈ dividend yield (≈1.3–1.5%/yr over 2021–26), a smooth, low-variance series. In `R_p − r_f = α + β(R_m − r_f) + e`, swapping price→TR adds ≈ȳ_div to the regressor mean with negligible variance change, so **β̂ is essentially unchanged** (slope shift second-order) and **α shifts by ≈ −β·ȳ_div**: at the internal |β|≤0.02 that is ≤3 bp/yr; even at β=0.10 it is ~14 bp/yr — immaterial against any real alpha claim, and beta *compliance* (§6 above) is unaffected. Where it is **not** immaterial: the cumulative context exhibit (§9.4 L978) — TR cumulates ≈ (1.014)^5.67 − 1 ≈ **8%** above price over the window (price cum. +104.6% 2021-01–2026-08 recomputed this session), so an unlabeled price-index line visibly flatters relative performance to any judge who benchmarks in TR, and Arcana's own market factor will not be the FRED price series.

**Frozen default:** if a total-return series is obtainable point-in-time (S&P 500 TR / CRSP value-weighted incl. distributions), use TR for alpha/beta and exhibits and say so; otherwise keep FRED SP500 but label every output "S&P 500 price index, excl. dividends" (repo precedent) and footnote the ≈β·ȳ_div alpha bound + ~8% cumulative context gap. Excess-return construction must also be named (Plan L353 omits it): `r_f` from the same TB3MS basis as §2 above, monthly /12 and daily /252. Event-day residual: which index the *judges/Arcana* regress on — reconcile after upload; expected discrepancy ≤ the bounds above.
