# v2: five improvements and the other switches

## Question
Can the submitted book (v1) be made cheaper to trade, steadier in market exposure and safer on the
short side without changing its signals? Each improvement is judged on the mechanism it targets,
never on information ratio (IR).

## Set-up (fixed before any run)
- Engine: `book.backtest(universe, optimize=v2.optimize)` on v1's universe, signals, costs
  (`costs.month_costs`), whole-share rounding and limits (`book.LIMITS`). v1 itself is never edited.
- Proof first: with every switch off and given v1's own drifted book from the month before, v2 must
  reproduce v1 on six sample months (2016-06, 2018-06, 2020-06, 2021-06, 2023-06, 2025-06): largest
  weight gap < 1e-4 and monthly net return within 0.01%. No v2 result is reported until this passes.
- Capital grid: $1M, $10M, $100M, $500M, $1B, $5B (NAV fed to the cost model). One trial per
  configuration; the grid is reported for each.
- Parameters below are fitted on data dated <= 2020-12 and then frozen.

## The five members
1. **Costs in the objective, in return units.** v1 maximizes a.w - 0.5 x ridge x sum(w^2)
   (`book.py:306`, ridge 30). v2 multiplies both terms by s_t = IC x median residual vol that month,
   which leaves v1's choice unchanged, then subtracts the convex cost of moving from the drifted
   book. IC = pooled residual IC over holding months 2015-01..2020-12; residual vol =
   `ivol_capm_252d` x sqrt(21) (monthly units). So a.w x s_t is a monthly return forecast (Grinold
   1994) and the cost is a monthly return: no free scaling. Per-stock Grinold alphas (IC x vol_i x
   z_i) are used only with a risk model (risk-sizing switch); without one they tilt toward volatile
   stocks. Convex cost = half spread x |dw| + sale fees x sells + impact x |dw|^1.5 + borrow on
   shorts + financing, from `costs.py` inputs; it must equal `month_costs` within 2% away from fee
   minimums. Mechanism: annual cost and turnover. Pass: cost and turnover fall in pre-2021 at every
   capital level, and the correlation between v2 and v1 weights stays >= 0.7.
2. **Gârleanu-Pedersen persistence weights.** Each of the 12 signals is weighted by
   1/(1 + phi_k x a/gamma): phi_k = 1 - mean one-month rank autocorrelation of signal k over
   2015-2020; a/gamma = (sqrt(1 + 4 lambda/gamma) - 1)/2 with both in monthly return units: gamma =
   ridge x mean s_t over 2015-2020, lambda = slope of a quadratic fit to the convex cost of v1's
   2015-2020 trades. a/gamma is computed once and frozen. Mechanism: turnover. Pass: turnover falls
   >= 10% in pre-2021 at $500M with v2-v1 weight correlation >= 0.7.
3. **Beta fix.** Two candidates for each stock's beta: `calibrated` (regress next-month realized
   daily beta on the LightGBM `beta_hat`, 2017-2020, apply the fitted line) and `welch` (Welch
   2019: 252 daily returns, each stock return winsorized between -2x and +4x that day's market
   return, weights halving every 126 trading days). Market = S&P 500 daily returns, as in v1's
   daily beta. The one with lower mean absolute error against next-month realized daily beta in
   2017-2020, averaged over long/short side x size group, is chosen and frozen. Fallback
   when inputs are missing: `beta_60m`, then `beta_dimson_21d`; never 0.
   Mechanism: beta forecast error and realized book beta. Pass: lower forecast error than v1's
   beta in pre-2021, and realized monthly beta closer to 0 than v1's in 2016-2020.
4. **Short rule.** No new short in: `size_grp` micro or nano; borrow fee >= 3% a year
   (`borrow.parquet`); `Parity_VSpread` in the bottom 5% of the month (options data, to 2025-08,
   then not applied); an 8-K with item 1.01, 2.01 or 8.01 filed in the 30 days to `eom` (item 3.02
   is not in our filings data). "New" means a name not short last month, or a short that grows;
   existing shorts may be held or reduced.
   Mechanism: borrow paid and short-side tail losses. Pass: borrow cost falls and the short
   leg's worst month in pre-2021 is no worse than v1's.
5. **Liquidity caps.** |w_i| x NAV <= 0.4 x `dolvol_126d` (two days at 20% of average daily dollar
   volume). Mechanism: cost at size. Pass: the cap binds as designed (max days to liquidate at 20%
   participation <= 2) and annual cost at $1B and $5B is below v1's.

v2 = all five members on. Each member is also run alone (five trials) so its own effect is shown.
Trials from this file: v2, five single members, four switches = 10.

## Other switches (reported beside v2, never part of it)
- **Institutional:** universe `me` >= $1,000M, NAV $1B, pool 480 names. Mechanism: cost and days
  to liquidate at $1B.
- **Aligned score:** v1 composite residualized each month on `beta_hat`, `risk_rank` and log
  `me`. Mechanism: the score's correlation with `risk_rank` (-0.51 before 2021) and size
  (+0.41). Pass: both within +/-0.1 in pre-2021.
- **Industry ranks:** signals ranked within `ff49` industries instead of demeaned by sector.
  Mechanism: industry exposure (sum of |net weight| by industry). Pass: falls in pre-2021.
- **Risk sizing:** per-stock Grinold alphas (IC x vol_i x z_i) with the ridge replaced by
  w' Sigma w from `risk.py`, scaled to the same average penalty over 2017. The risk model needs 24
  months of factor returns, so this starts 2017-01; 2016 months use v1's ridge. Mechanism: realized
  volatility per unit of gross return. Pass: lower than v1 over 2017-2020.

## Reported whatever happens
For v1, v2, each member and each switch: cost, turnover, realized beta (monthly and daily), days
to liquidate, borrow paid, and IR shown once for both windows, plus the capital grid. Failures
are reported next to passes.
