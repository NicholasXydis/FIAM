### Volume spike flag (PLAN §6.5, B24, B30)
- A stock-day is flagged when its volume exceeds 5 times the mean of the same stock's volume over the 126 sessions before it (that day excluded), with at least 63 such sessions; otherwise no flag.
- Split-safe volume:
  - CRSP (through 2025) and CA0065S: dollar volume, price times shares traded.
  - FINRA, which gives shares only: the mean uses only window days after the stock's most recent split before that day, again at least 63 of them. Split dates are CRSP distributions with a share factor (`disfacshr` > 0) through 2025. In 2026 they are the ends of FINRA short-interest periods whose `stockSplitFlag` is set; the split day inside such a period is unknown, so the whole period counts as before it.
- Test stock-days: 2015-01..2020-12, stocks whose panel `me` was at least $600m at the last `eom` before that day (lever A3).
- Pass: precision, the share of a source's flags that CRSP also flags, at least 0.8; recall, the share of CRSP's flags the source also flags, at least 0.8; and at least 95% of universe names covered on every 2026 trading day (counts only, §1.3).
- Order, never chosen by returns: CA0065S if bought and it passes, else FINRA if it passes, else the 2026 volume screens are off.

### FINRA short interest (PLAN §6.5)
- Symbols map to PERMNO by the CRSP ticker valid on the settlement date (`data/v4/links.parquet`). A class symbol ("BRK.B") maps by ticker plus CRSP share class; unmatched symbols are counted.
- 2026 coverage: the share of universe names with a FINRA short position on each 2026 settlement date. At least 95% on every date, else the stated fallback.
- WRDS agreement on 2021-06..2025-12 (counts only, §1.3): the share of matched (PERMNO, settlement) pairs whose FINRA short position equals WRDS `shortint` within 1% or 100 shares. Reported, not a gate.
- Amendment (approved by the leader 2026-10-23, after the agreement count above and before any short interest result was computed; no 2021+ returns seen):
  - The WRDS `short_interest` file is "Securities Finance short-interest / total-demand quantity" (stock lending; Stock Data README). FINRA agreed with it on 1.13% of 426,799 pairs, and with `short_interest_legacy`, the exchange series, on 99.96% of 338,475 pairs (2021-06..2024-12).
  - v4 uses exchange short interest only: the legacy file's `shortint` (never `shortintadj`) for settlements before 2021-06-01, FINRA's files from 2021-06-15, both public 8 sessions after settlement. The lending file is never read (registry `NEVER`).
  - FINRA rows with the revision flag are left out, as their first value is not in today's files (0.66% of rows). Two FINRA symbols on one PERMNO and date are both left out.
  - The mapping as built: the CRSP trading symbol, then the ticker, valid on the settlement date, letters and digits only (BRK.B = BRKB); a symbol two stocks share on a date stays unmapped and is counted.
  - The 95% coverage bar applies to every FINRA date from 2021-06 (98.06% minimum over 128 dates, counts only).

### French factor vintage (B23)
- Archive: the Internet Archive's newest copy, dated before 2021-01-01, of French's daily five-factor and momentum zips, fetched with a receipt by `tools/data/french.py archive`.
- Bar: on the overlap up to the archive's last day, at least 99% of daily values of UMD, and of each of the five factors, equal to today's copy or one rounding step apart, compared on the files' own two-decimal percent values (so float error cannot fail a match), measured by age. L is the shortest age after which the bar holds.
- Operational reading, written 2026-10-23 before the check ran: a value's age is the number of sessions from its date to the archive capture; for each age a, the bar is checked on all overlap values at least a sessions old; L is the smallest a at which it holds for all six series. Older pre-2021 captures are fetched too and reported beside, never deciding.
- Use: if L is 21 sessions or less, the crash control reads UMD only up to L sessions before each date. If L is longer, any factor fails, or no archived copy exists, the crash control stays off.

### FEMA and the Federal Register check (B1; written 2026-10-23 before the check ran)
- Data: OpenFEMA `DisasterDeclarationsSummaries` (v2) and `FemaWebDeclarationAreas` (v1), full CSV downloads with receipts (URL, SHA-256, rows, the dataset's `lastRefresh`), by `tools/data/fema.py`.
- Scope: major disasters (declaration type DR). A county's FEMA date is its earliest `designatedDate` in `FemaWebDeclarationAreas` for that disaster in an Individual or Public Assistance program (codes IA, IH, IFG, HA, DH, DUA, CC and PA with its categories). Hazard Mitigation (HM, usually statewide on the declaration day), Direct Federal Assistance, Other and SBA designations do not count. Corrected 2026-10-23 before the check ran: the first wording, "any program", would have dated every county from the statewide HM designation.
- Check 1: no county designated before its disaster's `declarationDate`; any that are get the declaration date.
- Check 2: 100 county designations drawn with seed 20261023 from DR county designations dated 1994-01-01..2020-12-31. For each, the Federal Register documents of FEMA naming "FEMA-<disaster number>-DR", oldest first; the notice date is the publication date of the first one whose text names the county (its name without the type suffix, as a whole word, any case) or has a sentence naming all counties or areas of the state for Individual or Public Assistance (the statewide Hazard Mitigation sentence does not count). Gap = notice date minus FEMA's date.
- Rule: if more than 5% of matched designations have a gap over 30 days, every FEMA date is replaced by its notice date where one exists and the rest count from event month + 4 months. If more than 20 of the 100 cannot be matched, the check is inconclusive and FEMA is not the fast clock (NOAA's event month + 4 months applies). Otherwise FEMA counts from the end of its date, county by county (§6.3).

- **Links before 2015 (decision 4A+).** After WRDS ratios, in this order and only for months ratios leaves empty: the stock's own 2015 panel gvkey and iid carried back from the gvkey's first Compustat date; Compustat's header CUSIP (`data/v4/extracts/compustat_ids.parquet`, linking only, never a signal) matched to CRSP's CUSIP of that month; then, for a stock whose every known link names one gvkey, that gvkey while it has Compustat data. - **Survivorship test (M3).** The six named firms are matched by name on any row; the earnings calendar's coverage is a projected release ahead at month end; an ex-ante gap (coverage at m, return at m+1) is reported beside the PDF's same-period test, never replacing it.

## Text section (step 4, written 2026-10-23 before any extraction ran)

### 10-K sections (Lazy Prices, customers, footprint)
- Source: the 56,665 annual reports (10-K, 10-KT) in `data/edgar/docs`, each the filing's main document; text is the HTML with tags removed and entities unescaped.
- A heading is "Item 1A" or "Item 7" followed within 40 characters by "Risk Factors" or "Management's Discussion", not preceded within 40 characters by "see", "in", "under", "refer" or "described", and not followed by a comma or quote. A section runs from its heading to the next heading of the next item (1B or 2 for 1A; 7A or 8 for 7). Of the candidate spans, the longest is kept. A span under 500 characters (for example "Not applicable") counts as missing.
- Boilerplate (K5): every sentence containing "forward-looking statement" or "forward-looking information" (any hyphen or a space between the words, from 2026-10-24, audit finding 13), "Private Securities Litigation Reform Act", "safe harbor" or "undue reliance" is removed before any similarity or tone is computed.
- Extraction bar: 100 annual reports drawn with seed 20261023 from 2015-2020 filings; a section is correct when it starts at its real heading and ends at the next item's heading. At least 90 of 100 correct for each of Item 1A and Item 7. Below that, the extractor is fixed once and checked on a fresh 100 (seed 20261024), both results logged; still below, Lazy Prices is recorded as failed, not built.
- Checker: the AI assistant reads each sampled filing around the extracted start and end and records correct or not with the evidence (`out/v4/text/hand_checks/`); the leader then checks 30 rows by hand (decision 9 below). Disclosed as AI-assisted checking.

### 8-K events and the 5.02 rule
- Events: one row per 8-K item per stock from the EDGAR index (`edgar_filings`), with its acceptance clock; 8-K/A rows are kept apart and never double-count.
- 5.02 counts as a red flag only for (a) a director leaving over a disagreement, or (b) the CEO or CFO (principal executive or financial officer) leaving: a sentence in the 8-K's text that names that role together with resign, retire, step down, depart, terminate, separate or "not stand for re-election". Appointments, elections and pay (5.02(c)-(f)) never count.
- Bar: 200 5.02 8-Ks drawn with seed 20261023 from 2015-2020; the rule must agree with the reading of the filing on at least 90%. Below that, fixed once and checked on a fresh 200 (seed 20261024); still below, 5.02 leaves the red-flag set and the other items (1.03, 2.06, 3.01, 4.01, 4.02) stand.

### Footprint
- Per annual report: the principal executive office's state and ZIP from the cover page, and every US state (and DC, Puerto Rico) named in Item 2 Properties, by name or (from 2026-10-24, audit finding 7) by its capital code after a comma or before a ZIP ("Evansville, IN", "Dallas TX 75201"); "Washington, D.C." is DC and "Kansas" before "City" is no state; weights equal across named states, the headquarters state counted once more (2 units, whether or not Item 2 names it). Dated by the filing's acceptance.
- Bar: 50 annual reports (seed 20261023, 2015-2020): the headquarters state right on at least 95% of them. Property states (added 2026-10-24, before any check): 50 annual reports drawn with seed 20261026 from 2015-2020 reports in no earlier draw; a blind reader lists the US states Item 2 names; a report is right when the rule adds no state and misses at most one; at least 45 of 50. Below that, fixed once and rechecked with seed 20261027; still below, the footprint uses the headquarters state alone.

### Named major customers
- A sentence naming a customer that "accounted for", "represented" or "comprised" at least 10% of revenue or sales, with its share. Names are matched to PERMNO by the CRSP name valid on the filing date after removing legal suffixes (Inc, Corp, Co, Ltd, LLC, plc, Holdings), exact first, else one candidate above a token overlap of 0.8; government and "one customer" mentions are kept unmatched. Weights are the stated shares.
- Bar: 100 extracted pairs (seed 20261023, 2015-2020): name right on at least 85%, and among matched names the PERMNO right on at least 90%. Below either, fixed once and rechecked on a fresh 100; still below, customer momentum is recorded as failed.

### The six gates (PLAN §7.4)
- Formations: month ends 2015-01..2020-10 (amended 2026-10-24, final audit 1: the t+1 label of 2020-11 ends on the close of 2021-01-04, so 2020-11 is dropped, never truncated); the label is month t+1's residual return under the t+1 label below, so the last label ends on 2020-12-01.
- Known by and labels (replaced 2026-10-24 after the step 4 audit, finding 9): an input counts at formation t when its `first_close` is on or before t's last trading day T (PLAN §6.3), for events and panel characteristics alike. Every label used by the gates, the ancillaries, the models and every pre-2021 choice starts at the close of T+1, the book's first trade, and runs to the close of the first trading day after month t+1's last, compounded from daily total returns (CRSP daily with delisting returns through 2025, PLAN §6.4); the residual regression (PLAN §8.2) is applied to it. The panel's own `ret_exc_lead1m` label is reported beside as robustness. So no fit, gate or choice is ever credited with the day from T to T+1 that the book cannot earn (PDF p15).
- Universe: the trading universe at t (security `me` at least $600m, price at least $5, US common stock, PLAN §3.1).
- Each month the signal is ranked to [-1, 1] within the universe, multiplied by its sign, then replaced by its residual from a cross-sectional OLS on the SYY-10 composite and the 147 characteristics (ranked, missing as 0); stocks without the signal are left out that month, except where a signal defines its own zero.
- A month counts with at least 100 stocks carrying the signal; a gate with fewer than 24 such months is recorded as failed for coverage.
- Statistic: the monthly Spearman correlation with the next-month residual return; its mean, one-sided t with Newey-West 6 lags; Holm step-down at 5% family-wise over the six (0.83%, 1%, 1.25%, 1.67%, 2.5%, 5%); a signal not built counts as failed (B4).
- Ancillary (K2), reported and never gating: the same monthly Spearman against the next as-filed EPS surprise announced within 4 months after t ((EPS_q - EPS_q-4) over the standard deviation of the last 8 seasonal differences, at least 6), formations to 2020-08; for Lazy Prices, against a red-flag 8-K in the next 12 months, formations to 2019-12; so every outcome window ends by 2020-12-31 and the gate code asserts it (PLAN §1.3: dropped, never truncated). IC by year is reported for each gate. A binary signal takes average ranks for ties.

### One gated construction per signal (variants are robustness only, reported after the gate)
- Short interest: the latest settlement's short interest over CRSP shares outstanding at that settlement (Boehmer-Huszar-Jordan 2010); sign negative. Variants: days to cover; the 3-month change.
- Red flags: 1 when the stock has a red-flag 8-K (1.03, 2.06, 3.01, 4.01, 4.02 as the SEC codes them; 5.02 never, leader 2026-10-24; 8-K/A never; an 8-K that also reports item 2.02 is an earnings 8-K and never counts, PLAN §7.3) with `first_close` in the 90 calendar days to the known-by date, else 0; a stock with no CIK link that month is missing, never 0; sign negative; no decay inside the window. Lerman-Livnat (2010) measure the drift from one day after the filing through 30, 60 and 90 calendar days. (Leader's choice 2026-10-24: 90 days.) Variants: 30 and 60 days; the count of flags. The leader removed 5.02 before any return test (2026-10-24), so no text reader feeds red flags; on the trading universe in 2015-2020 this leaves 1,182 stock-months with a filing against 5,106 with departures, so the signal is thin and its gate may fail, counted under Holm like any other.
- Insider buying: Cohen-Malloy-Pomorski classification (PLAN §7.3) on open-market purchases (P) and sales (S) of common stock in Forms 4 and 4/A, each from its own acceptance; roles counted are officers and directors (a filer that is only a 10% owner is out); the opportunistic net purchase ratio over trades with `first_close` in the 6 months to the known-by date (Lakonishok-Lee 2001), 0 with none; a stock with no CIK link that month is missing, never 0, since its insiders may file elsewhere (rule added 2026-10-24, before any gate); sign positive. Dollar variant: the same ratio on shares times price.
- Lazy Prices: tokens are lower-case alphabetic words of 2 or more letters; term-count vectors; the cosine of Item 1A and of Item 7 against the same CIK's previous annual report of the same form filed 300 to 500 days earlier, both items in both reports; the score is the mean of the two cosines, carried 12 months from `first_close` (Cohen-Malloy-Nguyen 2010); sign positive (a low similarity is bad). Variants: Jaccard; each item alone.
- 13F best ideas: each filer's latest 13F-HR with `first_close` on or before the known-by date (amendments from their own date, B13; VALUE units fixed, A11); holdings matched to PERMNO by dated CUSIP; filers with at least 20 matched holdings; tilt is the holding's portfolio weight minus the stock's weight in the value-weighted CRSP market at the 13F's period end; a filer's best idea is its top tilt (Cohen-Polk-Silli 2010; leader's choice 2026-10-24: the top 1); the signal is the share of a stock's 13F holders for which it is a best idea; sign positive. Variant: the other top-k.
- Customer momentum: the supplier's latest annual report with `first_close` on or before the known-by date and filed within 15 months; its matched customers (`customer_links`) weighted by their stated shares (a floor share at its floor); the signal is the share-weighted mean of the customers' month-t total return (Cohen-Frazzini 2008: the customers' last month); suppliers with no matched customer are left out; sign positive. Variant: equal weights. Not built: its customer names failed their check, so it counts as tested and failed under Holm (PLAN §7.4), and customer links are removed from every use (2026-10-24).

### SYY-10 and the models
- SYY-10: each anomaly's within-month rank over all panel stocks; the composite is the mean over the ten of sign times rank with missing as 0, eligible with at least 5 present, then ranked within the trading universe; equal weights (Stambaugh-Yu-Yuan 2012).
- Return model inputs: the 147 characteristics (the organisers' list less every banned field, PLAN §1.2), the SYY-10 composite, the SUE forecast and each passing gated signal, ranked within month, missing as 0, with no missingness flag of any kind (PLAN §1.2, merge rule 4; final audit 2): a missing value is the month's median rank, 0. Monotone +1 on the SYY-10 composite, each SYY part times its sign except `gp_at`, `at_gr1` and `ret_12_1`, the SUE forecast and each passing signal times its sign; every other input free.
- ElasticNet grid: alpha {0.0001, 0.001, 0.01} x l1_ratio {0.1, 0.5, 0.9}. Net (Gu-Kelly-Xiu NN3): 32-16-8 ReLU with batch norm, Adam 0.001, batch 10,000, L1 {0.00001, 0.0001, 0.001}, 30 epochs, 5 seeds averaged. Each chosen by the same 2019-2020 rule as the LightGBM grid.
- Confidence map: per stock, the standard deviation of the three models' rank forecasts; its terciles from every stock-date before the date (past-only, B21), applied once 12 months of forecasts exist (multiplier 1 before); multipliers 1, 0.9 and 0.75, fixed now.
- SUE gate: the mean monthly Spearman of the SUE forecast with the realised SUE of the next as-filed report within 4 months, over formations 2019-01..2020-08 so every outcome is public by 2020-12-31, above 0, its Newey-West t reported.
- B17 per characteristic: the mean monthly Spearman between the twin's and the panel's values on 2015-2020 at least 0.95; the twin trains a model only if every SYY-10 part passes; a failing characteristic is missing before 2015.

### Construction and the desk
- Derivation order, each at base gross C_t - 0.05: the alpha scale, the cost budget, ridge, b, d, the sector band (lever B1), lever A4's rate, then the brake gate (the crash control is off).
- Ridge grid {1, 3, 10, 30, 100, 300}, by the name-count rule (PLAN §8.3).
- Factor share for b: ex-ante x'BFB'x over x'Σx for the book's active weights, under our risk model and under the FF5+UMD model, median over 2017-2020 formations.
- Largest feasible gross: bisection from 1.00 to C_t in steps of 0.01, the largest gross at which the formation solve is optimal with every rule and constraint and no relaxation.
- Lever A4: each month's target is the drifted book plus r times the move to the optimum, r = 1 minus the mean month-to-month cross-sectional Spearman of the walk-forward pre-2021 forecasts (Garleanu-Pedersen 2013), kept within [0.2, 1]; the four-close spill then executes that target.
- Name-cap drift: a drifted weight above 1.2 times its cap at close t is trimmed to the cap at close t+1.
- Brake, v4 form of v3's rule: at 5% or more below the book's peak, gross falls to 0.625 times the formation gross (v3's 100 of 160) until half the fall is recovered; only if PLAN §8.3's autocorrelation test passes.
- Overlays (lever E5), only where the decay test passes (PLAN §8.7): a held long with a red flag, or a held short with an opportunistic insider purchase, is halved at the close after the event's `first_close`; one cut size, no grid.
- Lever B1: the sector band widens to +/-2% only if the median 2017-2020 ex-ante transfer coefficient rises by at least 0.03, and to +/-3% only by a further 0.03.
- Lever C3 churn (Gaspar-Massa-Matos 2005): a filer's quarterly churn is the sum over holdings of |N_t P_t - N_t-1 P_t-1 - N_t-1 (P_t - P_t-1)| over the sum of (N_t P_t + N_t-1 P_t-1) / 2, averaged over its previous four quarters; short-horizon filers are the top third; crowding is their summed shares over shares outstanding.
- Betas: the residual target, the alignment and the optimiser use Welch's slope-winsorised beta on 252 trading days (each excess return clipped to [-2, 4] times the market's, half-life 126 days, as `fiam/freeze.py`) and the Dimson beta on 126 trading days with one lead and one lag; the daily re-hedge trigger's "126-day beta" (PLAN §9.2) is Welch's on the last 126 trading days with a 63-day half-life.
- Fixed-rule guard (arm A1) shrink map: earnings within 5 days 0.75; borrow tier up 2 levels 0.5; more than 5 days to exit 0.75; a volatility jump 0.5; a loss beyond 3 sigma 0.5; several triggers multiply, floor 0.25; a constraint near its bound only explains.
- Near its bound: a constraint with less than 10% of its allowed width left, or a name at its cap.
- Memory gate: each card names the true company among k = 10 (it and 9 others from the same GICS-2 sector and month) and the month among 12; the chance band is the 95% binomial interval around 1/k; at least 200 cards.
- Kill criteria (reported when they would have fired, never applied): the trailing 252-day IR, (mean daily return less costs less the bill plus 4%) over the daily tracking error, both annualised, below -1 with the trailing factor share of active variance above 60%; or a drawdown deeper than twice the expected maximum drawdown of a Brownian motion with the ex-ante tracking error and an IR of 0.5 over a year (Magdon-Ismail et al. 2004) while the trailing 12-month 21-day forecast spread is below 0 with t below -1.
