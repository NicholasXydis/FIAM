  PICK ON THE DAY, NOT FROM THIS FILE. What follows is the requirement a model has to
  meet. Model names go stale in months, so whoever runs this chooses the best available
  option that clears the bar, benchmarks it on their own machine, and writes the hash in.

  size       Must fit in 16GB VRAM with its KV cache. At Q4_K_M that means roughly 14B:
             ~9GB of weights plus ~2GB of cache. A 27B at Q4 needs ~18GB, does not fit,
             and spills to CPU. A model that fits beats a larger one that does not.
  licence    Apache-2.0 or MIT only. A community licence with a use restriction is not
             worth arguing about in a submission, whatever the benchmark says.
  vendors    Two models from DIFFERENT vendors. Agreement between two models of the same
             lineage tells you nothing. This is the only reason we run two at all.
  excluded   Harmony models (gpt-oss-*). Their control tokens break the grammar sampler,
             and forcing valid JSON is what makes a run repeatable.
  sampling   temperature 0, seed 42, --grammar-file prompts/triage.gbnf. Same prompt gives
             the same answer every time, which is what lets a judge rerun it.
  running    One at a time on a 16GB card, never both at once.

  Qwen3 14B (Apache-2.0) is the strongest candidate we are aware of for model 1 and a
  sensible default. For model 2 the requirement is a DIFFERENT vendor, so check what is
  current at the time: Mistral, Gemma and Phi have all shipped models in this class under
  Apache-2.0 or MIT. NOTHING HERE HAS BEEN RUN OR BENCHMARKED. Pick on measured speed and
  output quality on the actual card, then write the hash in.
